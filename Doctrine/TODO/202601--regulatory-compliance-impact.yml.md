# Regulatory / compliance impact

- Compute by assessing risk of non-compliance or regulatory benefit.
- Key elements: legal requirements, audit findings, compliance deadlines.
- Measurement: legal review, audit reports, compliance checklists.
- Formula: Compliance_Impact = (Legal_Reqs * Weight1) + (Audit_Findings * Weight2)
