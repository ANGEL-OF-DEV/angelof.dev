# The Prime Directive

To exercise self-governing competences, this repository adopts
**directives**, **decisions** and **recommendations**.

> **Directive** is **binding as to the result to be achieved**,
> but gives free choice of form and methods of implementation.

> **Decision** is **binding in its entirety** within the scope in which it is enacted.

> **Recommendation** has no binding force, but must always be considered as an option.

To promote engagement and to allow stakeholders to understand how it operates,
this repository keeps records of ongoing activities and contributions together with satisfactory reasons for them.

> [Decision Log](./DecisionLog.md) keeps records of **directives**, **decisions** and **recommendations**.

> [Change Log](./ChangeLog.md) keeps records of **key contributions** and **significant changes** to this repository.
